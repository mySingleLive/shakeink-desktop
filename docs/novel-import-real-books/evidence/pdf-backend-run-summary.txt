Fixtures/implementation limits
- The legacy-PDF checkpoint fixture now includes frozen historical model grants
  with contextWindow 16000. Its earlier artificial completed facts plus models:{}
  correctly failed the newly approved budget policy; production rejection was
  preserved, not loosened. Checkpoint bytes/scope/facts remain asserted unchanged.
- The first postverify test expected exactly two read calls. The real snapshot
  performs an additional EOF check, so the fixture now captures readsAtHold and
  proves no further chain starts. No production change was needed for this.
- Family fallback permits only the two closed PDF.js warning patterns when
  relevant fonts are unembedded and every required codepoint has a nonzero glyph
  in the verified repository Noto font. Missing/corrupt fonts/CMaps, unknown
  diagnostics, image drops and decode failures remain blocked.
- Worker heap/pixel/operator/source/cache caps do not constitute a total native
  RSS guarantee. Resource/diagnostic/timeout ambiguity never emits a valid token.
